Kundali Reading Basics

An interactive Vedic astrology learning tool. See how a native's kundali overlays on the Kal Purush blueprint. Pick a lagna, place planets in houses, and watch the chart explain itself.

How to use
1 Pick your lagna from the dropdown. The native chart rotates so that sign sits in H1. The Kal Purush chart stays fixed with Aries in H1.
2 Place planets — click a planet chip, then click a house on the native chart. Rahu and Ketu place as a 180° axis. Click a placed planet to remove it.
3 Click any house to read it. The center panel explains the house through its natural sign (Kal Purush) and its actual sign (your lagna).
4 Switch views with the tabs. Drishti = aspects. Dispositor chain = each planet's sign-lord journey. Bhavat Bhavam = read any house as the new 1st.
5 Toggle C and R on placed planets. Mark a planet combust or retrograde to study how it changes the reading. Pills appear below each planet name in the panel.
6 Watch the badges on placed planets. ↑ Exalted  ·  ★ Mooltrikona  ·  ⌂ Own sign  ·  ↓ Debilitated  ·  C Combust  ·  R Retrograde
Tip: In Bhavat Bhavam mode, pick H7 as the base and click any house — you'll see what that house means for your spouse. The "Common readings" cards show classical interpretations like "10th from 7th = spouse's career."
